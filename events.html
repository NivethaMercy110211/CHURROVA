Venue Logistics

Simple Setup Requirements

Our stations are engineered to be self-sufficient and adaptable to ballrooms, historic venues, garden marquee tents, and urban rooftops.

  • Power:

    One standard 13A electrical socket (outdoor silent battery/generator option available on request).

  • Footprint:

    2.2m × 1.5m level surface area for the artisan cart plus chef serving space.

  • Access:

    Standard doorway clearance (80cm width). We carry ramp equipment for steps.

  • Certifications:

    £5M Public Liability Insurance, Food Safety Level 3 certified staff, PAT tested hardware.
