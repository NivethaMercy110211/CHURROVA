07 — Our Craft

Behind the Golden Crunch

Every churro we serve starts with a recipe we're proud of and hands that care. Here's how we bring the golden crunch to life.

1

The Perfect Batter

Flour, eggs, butter and just the right touch of salt — our signature batter recipe hasn't changed since we started.

2

Piped Fresh, Fried Hot

Each churro is piped directly into clean hot oil and fried to a perfect golden crisp — never pre-made, always fresh.

3

Filled & Finished

Filled churros get their creamy center piped in immediately after frying. Then tossed in cinnamon sugar by hand.

4

Served in Minutes

From fryer to plate in under 5 minutes. Hot, crispy, perfect — that's the CHURROVA promise.
