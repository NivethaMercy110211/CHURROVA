Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our dough, dips, catering service, and allergens.

Yes! Our core classic churro dough recipe is 100% plant-based made with premium wheat flour, filtered water, sea salt, and cold-pressed sunflower oil. Our Dark Venezuelan Chocolate dip and seasonal Berry Coulis are also fully vegan. Filled churros and dairy dips are clearly designated with allergen flags.
Always. We do not use heat lamps or pre-packaged fried stock. When you place an order, our pastry press extrudes fresh dough directly into 190°C high-stability oil, fry-crisped in 2.5 minutes, and dusted immediately in warm cinnamon sugar so it reaches you blisteringly crisp.
For peak summer wedding and festival season (May to September), we advise reaching out 6 to 12 weeks in advance. For birthday parties and corporate evening events during the autumn/winter, 2 to 3 weeks' notice is usually sufficient.
Yes! Our Sweet Sharing Boxes (12, 24, or 40 churro bites with 4 signature dips) can be pre-ordered online or by contacting your nearest location 24 hours prior. We pack them into custom insulated steam-vented boxes that keep them piping hot for your commute.
Because our kitchens handle traditional wheat flour continuously and share fryer filtration systems, we currently cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment for severe coeliac disease. We are developing a dedicated GF weekend pop-up schedule coming in late 2026!
