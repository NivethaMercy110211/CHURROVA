Our Story & Craft

Dough, Fire & Devotion

We set out to reinvent the traditional churro bar for the modern dessert lover: honoring historic Spanish star-die extrusions, elevating ingredients to Michelin-grade quality, and serving every single order blisteringly hot.

Born in 2021

The Churro Obsession

CHURROVA was born from an uncompromising realization: most churros served outside traditional Spanish chocolaterías were pale, frozen, limp imitations of what should be one of the culinary world's greatest sensory triumphs.

A proper churro is a study in thermal and textural contrast: an intensely crisp, deeply golden ridged shell dusted in fragrant cinnamon sugar, yielding to an airy, tender, custard-like interior — ready to plunge into bittersweet molten ganache.

We spent 18 months refining our scalding choux dough ratio, testing dozens of flours from regenerative UK and European mills, and designing custom star extruders that maximize surface crispness while retaining a cloud-soft center.

Restrained Sweetness

Our Flavor Philosophy

We deliberately reject the cloying, synthetic sweetness prevalent in industrial fast food. Street desserts deserve nuance.

Unsalted Cultured Butter & Sea Salt Balance

Every caramel and pastry cream features Cornish sea salt flakes to cut richness and highlight butteriness.

Direct-Trade Spice Roots

Our signature dusting uses wild harvested Korintje cinnamon blended with subtle green cardamom and coarse Demerara crystals.

Warmth, Freshness & Joy

Dessert should evoke communal delight — from spontaneous late-night walks to celebratory festival milestones.
